# Portfolio Glossary

## Value and Valuation Terms

**Market value.** The value of a position or account at closing prices on a given date, including cash and, where the firm includes it, accrued income.

**AUM (assets under management).** The total market value of the accounts a firm manages. In the portfolio domain, quarter-end AUM is the account's official market value on the last business day of the quarter; it is the figure handed to billing as billable AUM before any billing exclusions.

**Quarter-end valuation.** The locked, versioned market value of every managed account as of quarter end, used for drift, performance and billing.

## Allocation Terms

**Asset class.** A group of holdings with similar risk characteristics, such as US equity, International equity, Core bonds, Long-duration bonds, Alternatives or Cash. Drift and targets are measured at this level.

**Model portfolio.** A firm-defined, effective-dated set of target weights by asset class plus a drift tolerance, applied to many accounts.

**Target weight.** The percentage of an account's market value the model intends for an asset class. Target weights in a model sum to 100.

**Actual weight.** The asset class's market value divided by the account's total market value on the measurement date.

**Sleeve.** A sub-portfolio inside one custodial account that follows its own model or manager and has its own drift measurement.

## Drift and Rebalancing Terms

**Drift.** Actual weight minus target weight, in percentage points. Positive drift is overweight; negative drift is underweight.

**Tolerance band.** The range around each target weight, defined by the model's drift tolerance, within which no action is required. With a 5-point tolerance, a 20% target has a band from 15% to 25%.

**Outside tolerance.** The state of an account in which at least one asset class has absolute drift greater than the model's tolerance.

**Rebalance.** A set of trades that moves an account's actual weights back toward its target weights. A rebalance changes weights but not total market value.

## Grouping Terms

**Household.** A group of related accounts, such as those of one family or institution, viewed together for allocation, performance and reporting. Billing may also aggregate a household's AUM for fee breakpoints.

**Held-away asset.** An asset the household owns outside the firm's managed accounts; reported but not rebalanced.

## Performance Terms

**TWR (time-weighted return).** A return that removes the effect of external cash flows by linking sub-period returns, used to evaluate investment results.

**Money-weighted return.** An internal rate of return that reflects the timing and size of the client's flows.

**Net-of-fee return.** Return after deducting advisory fees charged during the period. Gross-of-fee return is before those fees.
