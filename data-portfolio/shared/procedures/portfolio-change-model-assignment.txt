Moving an account to a different model portfolio

Section 1: Prepare
1. Confirm the reason for the change (updated risk questionnaire, new investment policy statement, change in time horizon or income need).
2. Confirm the target model code exists for the firm and is active on the intended effective date.
3. Compare the current and target models' asset-class weights and drift tolerance, and note which asset classes will need to be bought or sold.
4. For taxable accounts, estimate realized gains of the transition and agree with the advisor whether to transition fully or in stages.

Section 2: Change the Assignment
1. Open the account and set the new model portfolio with the agreed effective date.
   The effective date should be a business day; drift is measured against the new targets from that date.
2. If the account is divided into sleeves, change only the sleeve whose model is changing.
3. Save and confirm the account now shows the new model code and tolerance.

Section 3: Transition the Holdings
1. Run the drift report for the effective date; expect most asset classes to show drift against the new targets.
2. Generate a rebalance proposal toward the new model and follow the standard approval process.
3. If the transition is staged for tax reasons, record the planned stages and dates in the account notes.

Section 4: Verify and Communicate
1. After execution, confirm the account is inside the new model's tolerance bands.
2. Confirm the model change did not alter the account's total market value beyond trading costs.
3. Update the client's performance report benchmark so it uses the new model's blended benchmark from the effective date.
4. Record the old model, new model, effective date and reason in the account notes.
