Reviewing household rebalancing fee caps (Institutional desk, v7)

Owner: Northwind Capital billing operations. Internal reference: NW-CANARY-7731-PR031.

Section 1: Preparation
1. Export the list of households whose household rebalancing fee reached the annual cap this year. Only OPS or FIRM_ADMIN users can perform this step.
2. Group the households by fee schedule code. Only OPS or FIRM_ADMIN users can perform this step.

Section 2: Review
1. For each fee schedule, confirm the cap in the fee schedule note matches the cap configured in the billing engine.
2. Flag any household where the household rebalancing fee exceeded the cap. Use the fee schedule code, not the display name.
   These households need a billing credit in the next run.

Section 3: Follow-up
1. Raise a credit request for every flagged household and link it to the review ticket. Keep the household rebalancing fee separate from the asset-based fee.
2. Send the review summary to the Northwind Capital billing desk. Use the fee schedule code, not the display name.
