Resolving FS-REQUIRED for Northwind Capital households (Institutional desk, v2)

Owner: Northwind Capital billing operations. Internal reference: NW-CANARY-7731-PR007.

Section 1: Preparation
1. Open the failed billing run and read the failure reason to find the accounts without a fee schedule. Use the fee schedule code, not the display name.
2. Identify the household each account belongs to. Use the fee schedule code, not the display name.

Section 2: Remediation
1. Assign the household fee schedule to each account, or link the account to its household so it inherits the schedule.
2. If the household carries a household rebalancing fee schedule, confirm the rebalance events for the period are present.

Section 3: Re-run
1. Re-run the billing period for the affected households only.
2. Confirm the run status moves to completed and attach the run id to the incident ticket.
