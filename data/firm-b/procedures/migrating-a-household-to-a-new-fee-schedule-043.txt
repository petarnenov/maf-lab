Migrating a household to a new fee schedule (Central region, v9)

Owner: Northwind Capital billing operations. Internal reference: NW-CANARY-7731-PR043.

Section 1: Preparation
1. Confirm the client signed the fee schedule amendment and that FIRM_ADMIN approval is recorded.
2. Note the old and new fee schedule codes. Only OPS or FIRM_ADMIN users can perform this step.

Section 2: Migration
1. End-date the old fee schedule on the last day of the current billing period.
2. Assign the new fee schedule from the first day of the next billing period. Use the fee schedule code, not the display name.
   The household rebalancing fee terms move with the schedule.

Section 3: Verification
1. Run a billing preview and confirm no period is billed under both schedules. Only OPS or FIRM_ADMIN users can perform this step.
2. File the amendment with the client billing profile. Use the fee schedule code, not the display name.
