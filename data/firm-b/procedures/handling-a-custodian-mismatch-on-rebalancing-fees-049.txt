Handling a custodian mismatch on rebalancing fees (Institutional desk, v10)

Owner: Northwind Capital billing operations. Internal reference: NW-CANARY-7731-PR049.

Section 1: Preparation
1. Pull the custodian fee deduction report for the billing period. Use the fee schedule code, not the display name.
2. Match each household rebalancing fee line to a custodian debit. Use the fee schedule code, not the display name.

Section 2: Investigation
1. For unmatched lines, check whether the custodian deducted the asset-based fee and rebalancing fee as one amount.
2. Contact the custodian service desk if a deduction is missing entirely. Keep the household rebalancing fee separate from the asset-based fee.

Section 3: Resolution
1. Record the reconciliation outcome against the billing run. Only OPS or FIRM_ADMIN users can perform this step.
2. Re-run the billing period only if the invoice amounts themselves were wrong.
