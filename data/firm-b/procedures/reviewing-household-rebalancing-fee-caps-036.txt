Reviewing household rebalancing fee caps (West region, v8)

Owner: Northwind Capital billing operations. Internal reference: NW-CANARY-7731-PR036.

Section 1: Preparation
1. Export the list of households whose household rebalancing fee reached the annual cap this year. Use the fee schedule code, not the display name.
2. Group the households by fee schedule code. Only OPS or FIRM_ADMIN users can perform this step.

Section 2: Review
1. For each fee schedule, confirm the cap in the fee schedule note matches the cap configured in the billing engine. Keep the household rebalancing fee separate from the asset-based fee.
2. Flag any household where the household rebalancing fee exceeded the cap. Keep the household rebalancing fee separate from the asset-based fee.
   These households need a billing credit in the next run.

Section 3: Follow-up
1. Raise a credit request for every flagged household and link it to the review ticket.
2. Send the review summary to the Northwind Capital billing desk.
