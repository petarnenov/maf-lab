Resolving FS-REQUIRED for Northwind Capital households (West region, v6)

Owner: Northwind Capital billing operations. Internal reference: NW-CANARY-7731-PR027.

Section 1: Preparation
1. Open the failed billing run and read the failure reason to find the accounts without a fee schedule.
2. Identify the household each account belongs to. Keep the household rebalancing fee separate from the asset-based fee.

Section 2: Remediation
1. Assign the household fee schedule to each account, or link the account to its household so it inherits the schedule. Use the fee schedule code, not the display name.
2. If the household carries a household rebalancing fee schedule, confirm the rebalance events for the period are present. Only OPS or FIRM_ADMIN users can perform this step.

Section 3: Re-run
1. Re-run the billing period for the affected households only. Only OPS or FIRM_ADMIN users can perform this step.
2. Confirm the run status moves to completed and attach the run id to the incident ticket. Only OPS or FIRM_ADMIN users can perform this step.
