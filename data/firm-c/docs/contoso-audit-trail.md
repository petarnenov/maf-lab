# Billing Audit Trail

## What Is Recorded

The TAMP platform records every billing action taken by Contoso Advisors users: schedule creation and edits, schedule assignments, run starts and re-runs, adjustments, credits, approvals, and period closures. Each record includes the user, the time, and the before-and-after values where applicable. The audit trail is read-only and cannot be edited by any role, including FIRM_ADMIN. The firm's compliance consultant uses the READ_ONLY role to review the trail during quarterly spot checks.

## Firm Supplements

### Billing Binder

In addition to the platform audit trail, the operations lead maintains a quarterly billing binder. The binder contains the unassigned-accounts report cleared before the run, the invoice review checklist, the custodian reconciliation, and approvals for any credits or waivers. The binder is filed electronically in the compliance folder.

### Exception Log

Any unusual event, such as a failed run, a rejected custodian debit, or a client dispute, is recorded in the exception log with its resolution. The log is reviewed at the monthly operations meeting.

## Retention

Contoso Advisors retains billing records, including the platform export of the audit trail and the binder, for at least six years. Records related to a client dispute are kept until the dispute is resolved and for six years afterward. The operations lead confirms each January that the prior year's records are complete.
