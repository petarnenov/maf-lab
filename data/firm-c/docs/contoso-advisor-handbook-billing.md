# Advisor Handbook: Billing Responsibilities

## What Advisors Own

Advisors at Contoso Advisors do not run billing, but they own the client conversations that make billing accurate. Advisors confirm the fee schedule and tier during onboarding, explain quarterly-in-advance billing, and document any client request that could trigger the household rebalancing fee. They also notify the operations desk of new accounts, terminations, and household changes such as marriage or divorce. Late notice from an advisor is the main cause of failed runs and billing corrections at the firm.

## Rebalancing Requests

### Documenting the Request

When a client asks for an out-of-cycle rebalance, the advisor records the request in the household notes with the date and the client's reason. If the rebalance affects two or more accounts, the advisor tells the client that a household rebalancing fee applies unless a waiver is granted.

### Requesting Waivers

Advisors may request a waiver for the first rebalance after onboarding, for hardship, or for a life event. The request goes to the operations lead through the billing queue, and the advisor tells the client only after it is approved.

## Answering Client Questions

Advisors use the client billing FAQ and the invoice to answer routine questions. Questions about specific amounts, refunds, or disputes are routed to the operations desk. Advisors never promise a credit before the operations lead approves it.
