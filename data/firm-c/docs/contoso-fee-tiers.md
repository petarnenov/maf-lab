# Flat Fee Tiers Under CONTOSO-FLAT-100

## Tier Structure

CONTOSO-FLAT-100 is a flat-fee schedule, but it defines several fixed fee tiers so that the fee reflects the complexity of the household's planning needs rather than its asset level. Each Contoso Advisors household is assigned exactly one tier at onboarding. The tier determines the annual fee, and the annual fee is divided into four quarterly installments billed in advance. Tier assignment is reviewed at the annual planning meeting, and any tier change takes effect at the start of the next quarter.

## Tier Descriptions

### Essential Tier

The Essential tier covers households with straightforward needs: a single income, a few accounts, and no business ownership or complex estate planning. The fee covers investment management, the semi-annual review, and an annual planning update.

### Comprehensive Tier

The Comprehensive tier covers households with multiple income sources, equity compensation, rental property, or more involved tax planning. It includes quarterly meetings and coordination with the client's accountant.

### Family Office Tier

The Family Office tier is reserved for a small number of households with trusts, closely held businesses, or multi-generational planning. It includes coordination with estate attorneys and consolidated reporting across entities.

## Changing Tiers

Advisors propose tier changes in writing, and the operations lead updates the tier on the household record. The client receives a letter confirming the new annual fee at least 30 days before the change takes effect. Downgrades take effect immediately at the next run.
