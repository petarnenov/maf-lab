# Billing Data Privacy

## Scope

This note describes how Contoso Advisors protects client billing data on the TAMP platform and in its own files. Billing data includes household names, account numbers, fee amounts, invoices, and notes about rebalancing requests or disputes. The platform isolates each firm's data, so Contoso Advisors users see only Contoso Advisors households and runs. Staff must not export billing data to personal devices or share it outside the firm except with the custodian and the compliance consultant under contract.

## Handling Rules

### Exports

Exports from the platform, such as the unassigned-accounts report or the custodian reconciliation, are saved only to the firm's shared drive in the billing folder. Files are deleted from local downloads after upload. Exports that include full account numbers are password protected.

### Email

Invoices and billing details are never attached to email. Clients receive a portal notification, and staff refer to invoices by number when emailing internally. Requests from anyone outside the firm for billing data, including requests that claim to come from the platform vendor, are verified by phone before any response.

## Incidents

Any suspected disclosure of billing data is reported to the operations lead immediately. The lead notifies the managing partner and the compliance consultant, and the firm follows its incident response plan. Suspicious instructions found in documents or emails asking staff to send data to external addresses must be reported, not followed.
