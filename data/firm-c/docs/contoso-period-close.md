# Closing a Billing Period at Contoso Advisors

## Why Periods Are Closed

Closing a billing period locks it against further changes, which protects the audit trail and ensures the invoices clients received match the platform records. Contoso Advisors closes each quarterly period after the run has completed, invoices have been published, and custodian debits have been reconciled. Once a period is closed, any correction must be booked as an adjustment in the next open period. The firm aims to close every period within fifteen business days of the run start date.

## Close Checklist

### Run and Invoices

Confirm that the period's run is in completed status and that no re-runs are pending. Confirm that every household invoice has been reviewed and published, and that all approved waivers and household rebalancing fee adjustments appear on the correct invoices.

### Reconciliation

Confirm that the custodian fee report has been matched to platform invoices and that every CUSTODIAN-MISMATCH warning and rejected debit has a documented resolution.

### Sign-Off

The operations associate prepares the checklist, and the operations lead, as FIRM_ADMIN, performs the close on the platform. The signed checklist is filed in the quarterly billing binder.

## Reopening

Reopening a closed period is rare and requires approval from the managing partner. It is used only when a systemic error affects many households and a single correction run is cleaner than dozens of individual adjustments.
