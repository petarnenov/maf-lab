# Household Definition at Contoso Advisors

## What a Household Is

At Contoso Advisors a household is the billing unit for the flat fee. A household normally includes a client, the client's spouse or domestic partner, and any dependent children, together with every account they own at the custodian that Contoso Advisors manages. Revocable trusts and custodial accounts for minors belong to the household of the grantor or custodian. Because CONTOSO-FLAT-100 charges per household, correct household membership directly determines how many flat fees a family pays.

## Membership Rules

### Adding Members

Adult children who are financially independent form their own household, even if their parents pay their fee. If the parents want to pay, the adult child's household is billed under its own schedule and the fee can be debited from a parent's account with written authorization.

### Splitting Households

When a couple divorces or separates, the household is split at the start of the next quarter. Each new household receives its own schedule assignment and first invoice. The prior quarter's prepaid fee is not refunded because the service was delivered to the combined household.

## Platform Setup

Households are created on the TAMP platform by the OPS or FIRM_ADMIN user. The household record holds the schedule assignment, the flat fee tier, the rebalancing fee waiver history, and the agreement date. Account-level schedule overrides are not used at Contoso Advisors, so all accounts in a household share one schedule.
