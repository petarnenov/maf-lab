# Billing Setup for New Contoso Advisors Households

## Before the First Run

When a new household signs with Contoso Advisors, the operations associate creates the household on the TAMP platform, links every custodial account, and assigns a fee schedule. New clients are placed on CONTOSO-FLAT-100 unless their agreement names a different schedule. The associate records the annual fee tier and the agreement signature date on the household record. The signature date determines the proration start for the first invoice, so it must match the date on the signed agreement exactly.

## Account Linking

### Custodian Accounts

Each account opened at the custodian must be linked to the household before the next quarterly run. An unlinked account is billed as its own household, which would double-charge the flat fee. The associate confirms the link by checking that the household account count on the platform matches the count on the new account paperwork.

### Joint and Trust Accounts

Joint accounts and revocable trust accounts belong to the household of the primary client. Irrevocable trusts with a separate trustee may be set up as their own household if the agreement says so. The advisor confirms the treatment in writing during onboarding.

## First Invoice

The first invoice covers the partial quarter from the signature date to the end of the current quarter, plus the full next quarter if the account was funded before the run date. The operations lead reviews every first invoice before it is released. The client receives a welcome letter explaining how the flat fee will appear on custodian statements.
