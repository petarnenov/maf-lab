Producing the Annual Fee Summary

Section 1: Preparation
1. Confirm that the Q4 billing period of the prior year is closed.
2. Confirm that all credits and adjustments for the year have been applied.

Section 2: Generate
1. Run the annual fee summary report for all Contoso Advisors households on the platform.
2. Spot-check five households against their invoices for the year.
   Include at least one household with a household rebalancing fee and one with a termination credit.

Section 3: Deliver
1. Publish the summaries to the client portal by January 31.
2. Notify advisors that summaries are available so they can answer questions.
