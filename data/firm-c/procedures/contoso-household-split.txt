Splitting a Household

Section 1: Preparation
1. Obtain written instructions from both clients or a copy of the separation agreement.
2. Determine which accounts go to each new household.
3. Confirm that the split takes effect at the start of the next quarter.

Section 2: Platform Changes
1. Create the second household on the platform with its own agreement date.
2. Move the relevant accounts to the new household with the effective date.
3. Assign CONTOSO-FLAT-100 and the correct tier to each household.

Section 3: Verify
1. Confirm that no accounts are left without a schedule.
2. Flag both households for first-invoice review in the next run.
3. Note that the prior quarter's prepaid fee is not refunded after a split.
