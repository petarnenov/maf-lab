Issuing a Billing Credit at Contoso Advisors

Section 1: Justify the Credit
1. Identify the invoice and household that the credit corrects.
2. Document the reason, such as over-billing, a missed waiver, or a termination refund.
3. Calculate the credit amount and attach the calculation to the request.

Section 2: Approve
1. Enter the credit adjustment on the household as an OPS user.
2. Route the credit to the operations lead for FIRM_ADMIN approval if it exceeds the firm threshold.
3. Do not tell the client about the credit until it is approved.

Section 3: Apply
1. Confirm the credit appears in pending adjustments for the next open billing period.
2. If the correction is urgent, ask the operations lead to process it in an off-cycle run.
3. After the run, confirm the credit line on the invoice and notify the advisor.
