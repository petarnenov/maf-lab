Closing a Quarterly Billing Period

Section 1: Checks
1. Confirm that the run for the period is completed and no re-runs are pending.
2. Confirm that all invoices have been reviewed and published to the client portal.
3. Confirm that the custodian reconciliation is complete and every exception has a resolution.

Section 2: Close
1. Complete the period close checklist and sign it as the preparer.
2. Ask the operations lead to review the checklist and close the period on the platform.
3. Confirm that the period status shows closed.

Section 3: Afterward
1. File the signed checklist in the quarterly billing binder.
2. Route any correction found after close to the next open period as an adjustment.
