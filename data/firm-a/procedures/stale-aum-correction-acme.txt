Correcting a stale AUM valuation at Acme Wealth Partners

Section 1: Identify
1. Open the stale-valuation report for the billing period.
2. Note each account, its custodian, and the date of the last loaded position.
3. For legacy custodian trust accounts, allow up to seven business days of staleness before treating the account as stale.

Section 2: Correct
1. For primary custodians, request a position reload for the missing dates through the custodian feed console.
2. For the legacy custodian, upload the quarter-end statement manually and attach the PDF to the account record.
3. For alternative assets without a current statement, carry forward the prior value and flag it.
   If the carried-forward value exceeds $1,000,000, request FIRM_ADMIN review before proceeding.

Section 3: Confirm
1. Refresh the AUM valuation for the affected accounts.
2. Re-run the stale-valuation report and confirm it is empty.
3. Re-run the failed billing run if it had failed with AUM-STALE.
