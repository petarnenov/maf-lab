Splitting a household at Acme Wealth Partners

Section 1: Preparation
1. Obtain written confirmation from the advisor of which accounts belong to each new household.
2. Confirm which fee schedule each new household will use.
3. Choose an effective date equal to the next billing period start.

Section 2: Perform the Split
1. Create the new household record and move the confirmed accounts into it.
2. Assign the agreed fee schedule to every account in the new household.
   A new household without a schedule will cause an FS-REQUIRED failure.
3. Update the grouping notes on both households.

Section 3: Validate
1. Run billing previews for both households.
2. Confirm that tiered fees are now calculated separately for each household.
3. Notify the billing desk at Acme of the split so the approval threshold impact can be monitored.
