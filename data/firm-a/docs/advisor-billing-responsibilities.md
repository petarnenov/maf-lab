# Advisor Billing Responsibilities

## Onboarding New Households

Advisors at Acme Wealth Partners are responsible for making sure every new household has a signed advisory agreement, a fee disclosure matching ACME-TIER-2026 or a documented exception, and correct household grouping before the first billing period ends. OPS configures the accounts, but the advisor confirms that the configuration matches what the client agreed to.

## Ongoing Duties

### Quarterly Review

Before each quarterly run, advisors review the billing preview for their households. They look for accounts missing a schedule, unusual changes in AUM, and credits that were promised but not entered. Issues must be raised with OPS before business day four.

### Client Questions

Advisors are the first point of contact for fee questions. If a client disputes an invoice, the advisor gathers the details and opens a ticket with the billing desk rather than promising a credit directly.

## What Advisors Cannot Do

Advisors hold the ADVISOR role, which cannot approve invoices over $25,000, change fee schedule definitions, or close billing periods. Those actions require FIRM_ADMIN or OPS permissions, and attempts to work around them are reported to compliance.
