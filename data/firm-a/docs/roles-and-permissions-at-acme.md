# Roles and Permissions at Acme Wealth Partners

## Role Assignments

Acme Wealth Partners uses the four standard platform roles. The mapping of staff to roles is reviewed every quarter by the FIRM_ADMIN group and recorded in the access review log.

### FIRM_ADMIN

Held by the Chief Operating Officer, the Head of Operations, and two billing desk supervisors. FIRM_ADMIN users approve invoices over $25,000, edit ACME-TIER-2026 and other schedules, approve fee exceptions, and close billing periods.

### OPS

Held by the billing desk analysts. OPS users assign schedules, start and re-run billing runs, correct stale valuations, and reconcile custodian deductions.

### ADVISOR

Held by client-facing advisors. Advisors see their own households, review billing previews, and enter credits up to $1,000.

### READ_ONLY

Held by compliance reviewers and auditors. READ_ONLY users can view runs, invoices and schedules but cannot change anything.

## Access Changes

Role changes are requested through the billing desk and approved by a FIRM_ADMIN. Departing staff lose access on their last working day, and any pending approvals assigned to them are reassigned to another FIRM_ADMIN.
