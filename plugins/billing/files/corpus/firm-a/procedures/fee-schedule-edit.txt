Editing a fee schedule definition at Acme Wealth Partners

Section 1: Approval
1. Confirm the change proposal has been reviewed by compliance.
2. Confirm two FIRM_ADMIN users have approved the proposal.

Section 2: Implement
1. As a FIRM_ADMIN, open the fee schedule and create a new version rather than editing the current one.
2. Enter the new bands and rates, and set the effective date to the first day of a future billing period.
3. Save the version and confirm the prior version remains active until the effective date.

Section 3: Verify
1. Run billing previews for a sample of at least five affected households.
2. Compare the previews with the proposal and stop the rollout if any fee differs unexpectedly.
3. File the approvals and preview evidence in the operations archive.
