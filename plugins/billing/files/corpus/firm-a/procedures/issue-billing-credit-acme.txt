Issuing a billing credit at Acme Wealth Partners

Section 1: Validate the Request
1. Confirm the reason for the credit: overcharge, service concession, or missed fee waiver.
2. Recalculate the correct fee using the schedule on file and the valuation used in the run.
3. Determine the credit amount as the difference between the invoiced and correct fees, or the agreed concession amount.

Section 2: Approvals
1. Credits up to $1,000 may be entered by the advisor of record.
2. For credits above $1,000, notify the billing desk at Acme before entering the credit.
3. For credits above $5,000, obtain FIRM_ADMIN approval and record the approver's name.

Section 3: Enter the Credit
1. Create the credit on the household with the reason code, the original run id, and a client-facing explanation.
   The explanation must be plain language without internal codes.
2. Choose whether the credit applies to the next invoice or is refunded through the custodian.
3. Confirm the credit appears in the billing preview for the next period.
