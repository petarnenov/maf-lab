Assigning ACME-TIER-2026 to a household

Section 1: Confirm Eligibility
1. Check the fee exceptions register to confirm the household has no approved exception.
2. Confirm the client has signed the ACME-TIER-2026 fee disclosure.
3. Confirm the effective billing period start date with the advisor.

Section 2: Assign
1. Open the household and select every account in the household.
2. Set the fee schedule to ACME-TIER-2026 with the agreed effective date.
   The effective date must be the first day of a billing period.
3. Save and confirm each account shows the new schedule code.

Section 3: Verify
1. Run a billing preview for the next billing period.
2. Check that the household fee matches the tier table and the $2,500 minimum.
3. Record the assignment date in the household notes.
