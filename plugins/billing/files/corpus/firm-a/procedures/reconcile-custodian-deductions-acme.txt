Reconciling custodian fee deductions at Acme Wealth Partners

Section 1: Gather Data
1. Download the fee deduction confirmations from each primary custodian.
2. Obtain the processed instruction letters from the legacy custodian.
3. Export the invoiced amounts per account from the completed billing run.

Section 2: Match
1. Match each deduction to its invoice by account number and amount.
2. Flag any difference greater than $5 as a CUSTODIAN-MISMATCH exception.
3. Flag any invoice with no corresponding deduction, and any deduction with no invoice.

Section 3: Resolve
1. For under-deductions, submit a corrective fee instruction to the custodian.
2. For over-deductions, issue a billing credit or request a custodian reversal.
3. Document each exception and its resolution in the reconciliation workpaper and notify the billing desk.
