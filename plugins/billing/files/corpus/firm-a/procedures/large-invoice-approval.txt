Approving invoices over $25,000

Acme Wealth Partners requires FIRM_ADMIN approval for any invoice over $25,000. This procedure is performed by FIRM_ADMIN users after each billing run.

Section 1: Review
1. Open the Awaiting Approval queue for the completed billing run.
2. For each invoice, open the household detail and check the AUM valuation date and amount.
   Compare the AUM with the prior quarter and investigate changes greater than 20 percent.
3. Confirm that the fee schedule applied is ACME-TIER-2026 or matches the fee exceptions register.
4. Review any credits and minimum-fee adjustments on the invoice.

Section 2: Decision
1. If you are the advisor of record for the household, reassign the approval to another FIRM_ADMIN.
2. If everything is correct, approve the invoice and add a short approval comment.
3. If something is wrong, reject the invoice with a comment describing the correction OPS must make.

Section 3: Follow-up
1. Confirm approved invoices appear in the next supplemental custodian file.
2. Track rejected invoices until OPS re-runs or adjusts them, then review them again.
