Closing a billing period at Acme Wealth Partners

Section 1: Readiness
1. Confirm every billing run for the period is in completed status.
2. Confirm the approval queue is empty for the period.
3. Confirm custodian reconciliation is finished and every CUSTODIAN-MISMATCH exception has a documented resolution.

Section 2: Close
1. A FIRM_ADMIN opens the billing period page and selects Close Period.
2. Enter the close comment, including the number of runs and the total invoiced amount.
3. Confirm the period status changes to closed.

Section 3: Archive
1. Export the run reports, approval log and reconciliation workpapers.
2. File the exports in the operations archive under the period name.
3. Notify the billing desk and advisors that the period is closed and that further corrections require credits.
