# Migrating Households from ACME-TIER-2022 to ACME-TIER-2026

## Background

Before 2026, most Acme Wealth Partners households were billed on ACME-TIER-2022, a four-band tiered schedule with a higher top-band rate. The firm's investment committee approved ACME-TIER-2026 in late 2025 to lower fees for households above $3,000,000 and to simplify the minimum-fee rule. Migration happens household by household at the annual review, so both schedules will coexist on the platform throughout 2026.

## Migration Rules

### Timing

A household moves to ACME-TIER-2026 effective on the first day of the billing period following its annual review. Mid-period schedule changes are not allowed, because they would force the platform to split the period and could produce a PRORATION-GAP failure if the split dates are entered incorrectly.

### Client Communication

Advisors must send the client the updated fee disclosure at least 30 days before the effective date. The disclosure template is stored in the advisor resource library and must not be edited without compliance review.

## Operational Checklist

OPS updates the schedule assignment on each account in the household, confirms that every account shows the same schedule code, and runs a billing preview for the upcoming period. If the preview shows any account without a schedule, the migration is paused and the billing desk is notified. Once the preview is clean, the advisor records the migration date in the household notes.
