# Adjustments and Credits at Acme Wealth Partners

## When Credits Are Issued

Acme Wealth Partners issues billing credits when a client was overcharged, when a service failure justifies a goodwill concession, or when a fee waiver was promised during onboarding and not configured in time. Credits are never used to correct a wrong fee schedule assignment in the current open period; in that case the schedule is corrected and the run is re-executed instead.

## Approval Limits

### Small Credits

Credits up to $1,000 may be entered by an ADVISOR for their own households and are reviewed by OPS during period close.

### Large Credits

Credits above $1,000 must be reported to the billing desk, and credits above $5,000 require FIRM_ADMIN approval. A credit that reduces an invoice from above $25,000 to below $25,000 does not remove the need for FIRM_ADMIN approval of that invoice, because the approval requirement is evaluated before credits are applied.

## Recording Requirements

Every credit must include a reason code, the original run id, and a short free-text explanation. The explanation appears on the client invoice, so it must be written in plain language and must not include internal reference codes or account numbers belonging to other clients.
