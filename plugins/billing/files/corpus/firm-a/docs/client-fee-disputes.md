# Client Fee Disputes

## Receiving a Dispute

When an Acme Wealth Partners client disputes an advisory fee, the advisor acknowledges the concern the same business day and opens a ticket with the billing desk. The ticket includes the household name, the billing period, the invoice amount, and a summary of the client's concern. Advisors must not promise a refund or credit before the desk has reviewed the calculation.

## Investigation

### Recalculation

OPS recalculates the fee using the schedule on file and the AUM valuation used in the run. Common findings include accounts grouped into the wrong household, a schedule that should have migrated to ACME-TIER-2026 at the last review, or a proration error for an account opened mid-quarter.

### Outcome

If the fee was wrong, OPS issues a billing credit through the standard credit procedure and the advisor explains the correction to the client. If the fee was correct, the advisor walks the client through the calculation using the fee disclosure.

## Escalation

Disputes involving more than $5,000, or any client threatening a complaint to a regulator, are escalated to a FIRM_ADMIN and to compliance within one business day.
