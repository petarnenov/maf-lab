# Fee Schedule Change Control

## Scope

This policy covers changes to the definition of any Acme Wealth Partners fee schedule, including ACME-TIER-2026, legacy schedules and negotiated exception schedules. It does not cover assigning an existing schedule to an account, which OPS may do as part of routine setup.

## Change Requirements

### Proposal

A proposed change must describe the new rates or bands, the households affected, the effective billing period, and the client communication plan. The proposal is reviewed by compliance for disclosure impact.

### Approval

Two FIRM_ADMIN users must approve every schedule definition change. The change is scheduled to take effect at the start of a billing period, never mid-period, and at least 30 days after client notices are sent.

## Implementation

A FIRM_ADMIN enters the new schedule version on the platform with a future effective date. OPS runs billing previews for a sample of affected households and compares the result with the proposal. Any unexpected difference stops the rollout until the cause is found. The billing desk records the change, approvals and preview evidence in the operations archive.
