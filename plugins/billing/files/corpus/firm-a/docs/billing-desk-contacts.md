# Acme Billing Desk

## Role of the Billing Desk

The billing desk at Acme Wealth Partners is the operational team that owns firm-specific billing configuration, fee schedule assignments, and escalation of failed billing runs. The desk sits within the Operations group and is staffed by OPS users, with two FIRM_ADMIN supervisors who approve large invoices and schedule changes. Every failed billing run, missing fee schedule, or client fee dispute must be reported to the desk so that it can be tracked in the firm's internal billing log. Internal reference code: ACME-CANARY-4410.

## When to Notify the Desk

### Mandatory Notifications

Advisors and OPS users must notify the billing desk at Acme whenever a billing run fails with FS-REQUIRED, AUM-STALE, CUSTODIAN-MISMATCH or PRORATION-GAP; whenever a fee schedule is assigned or changed outside the annual review cycle; and whenever a billing credit above $1,000 is proposed. Notification is made through the billing desk queue in the firm's ticketing system, with the run id and affected account numbers.

### Optional Notifications

Routine questions about invoice formatting, statement timing or household grouping can be sent to the desk, but they are not tracked in the billing log and are answered on a best-effort basis within two business days.

## Service Levels

The desk acknowledges mandatory notifications within four business hours during the billing window, which runs from the first to the tenth business day after quarter end. Outside the billing window, acknowledgement is within one business day.
