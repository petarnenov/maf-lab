# Invoice Presentation Standards

## Invoice Layout

Every Acme Wealth Partners client invoice shows the billing period, the household name, each account with its average AUM, the fee schedule code applied, and the resulting fee per account. The household total appears at the bottom, followed by any credits and minimum-fee adjustments. The Acme logo and the firm's regulatory disclosures appear in the footer.

## Wording Rules

### Schedule Description

Invoices show the schedule code ACME-TIER-2026 together with a plain-language description, such as "tiered annual rate from 1.00% to 0.45% based on household assets". Rate tables are not reproduced on the invoice; clients are referred to their fee disclosure.

### Credits

Credit lines use the client-facing explanation entered by the advisor or OPS. Internal reason codes, ticket numbers and run ids are removed before the invoice is rendered.

## Delivery

Invoices are delivered through the client portal on the day the fee deduction file is submitted. Invoices awaiting FIRM_ADMIN approval are not published until approval is recorded, so clients never see an invoice that might still change.
