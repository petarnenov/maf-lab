# Acme Wealth Partners Billing Glossary

## Firm-Specific Terms

### ACME-TIER-2026

The standard tiered fee schedule for Acme Wealth Partners households from January 2026. Rates step down from 1.00% to 0.45% across four asset bands with a $2,500 annual minimum.

### ACME-TIER-2022

The prior standard schedule, still assigned to households that have not yet had their 2026 annual review.

### Billing Desk

The Acme operations team that owns firm-specific billing configuration, approvals tracking and failure escalation.

### Approval Queue

The list of invoices over $25,000 waiting for FIRM_ADMIN approval before release to custodians and clients.

## Usage Notes

Platform terms such as AUM, breakpoint, proration, household, billing period, arrears and advance keep their platform definitions at Acme. Where Acme staff use informal terms, such as "big invoice" for an invoice over the approval threshold or "tier sheet" for the ACME-TIER-2026 rate table, written documentation should use the formal terms above so that searches and audits find the right records.
