Resolving FS-REQUIRED for Northwind Capital households (East region, v3)

Owner: Northwind Capital billing operations. Internal reference: NW-CANARY-7731-PR012.

Section 1: Preparation
1. Open the failed billing run and read the failure reason to find the accounts without a fee schedule. Only OPS or FIRM_ADMIN users can perform this step.
2. Identify the household each account belongs to. Only OPS or FIRM_ADMIN users can perform this step.

Section 2: Remediation
1. Assign the household fee schedule to each account, or link the account to its household so it inherits the schedule. Only OPS or FIRM_ADMIN users can perform this step.
2. If the household carries a household rebalancing fee schedule, confirm the rebalance events for the period are present. Use the fee schedule code, not the display name.

Section 3: Re-run
1. Re-run the billing period for the affected households only. Use the fee schedule code, not the display name.
2. Confirm the run status moves to completed and attach the run id to the incident ticket.
