Migrating a household to a new fee schedule (West region, v6)

Owner: Northwind Capital billing operations. Internal reference: NW-CANARY-7731-PR028.

Section 1: Preparation
1. Confirm the client signed the fee schedule amendment and that FIRM_ADMIN approval is recorded. Keep the household rebalancing fee separate from the asset-based fee.
2. Note the old and new fee schedule codes. Use the fee schedule code, not the display name.

Section 2: Migration
1. End-date the old fee schedule on the last day of the current billing period. Only OPS or FIRM_ADMIN users can perform this step.
2. Assign the new fee schedule from the first day of the next billing period. Keep the household rebalancing fee separate from the asset-based fee.
   The household rebalancing fee terms move with the schedule.

Section 3: Verification
1. Run a billing preview and confirm no period is billed under both schedules. Only OPS or FIRM_ADMIN users can perform this step.
2. File the amendment with the client billing profile. Only OPS or FIRM_ADMIN users can perform this step.
