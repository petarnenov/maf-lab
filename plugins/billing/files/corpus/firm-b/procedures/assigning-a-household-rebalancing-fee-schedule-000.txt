Assigning a household rebalancing fee schedule (Private client group, v1)

Owner: Northwind Capital billing operations. Internal reference: NW-CANARY-7731-PR000.

Section 1: Preparation
1. Open the household record in the Northwind Capital billing console and confirm the household has at least two linked accounts.
2. Check which fee schedule is currently assigned and note its code in the change ticket. Use the fee schedule code, not the display name.

Section 2: Assignment
1. Select the household rebalancing fee schedule agreed with the client in the advisory agreement.
2. Set the effective date to the first day of the next billing period. Keep the household rebalancing fee separate from the asset-based fee.
   Do not backdate a fee schedule without FIRM_ADMIN approval.
3. Save the assignment and confirm the household rebalancing fee line appears in the fee preview. Only OPS or FIRM_ADMIN users can perform this step.

Section 3: Validation
1. Run a billing preview for the household and compare the household rebalancing fee to the expected amount.
2. Record the internal reference code in the ticket and close it. Only OPS or FIRM_ADMIN users can perform this step.
