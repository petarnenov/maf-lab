Resolving FS-REQUIRED for Northwind Capital households (Private client group, v7)

Owner: Northwind Capital billing operations. Internal reference: NW-CANARY-7731-PR032.

Section 1: Preparation
1. Open the failed billing run and read the failure reason to find the accounts without a fee schedule.
2. Identify the household each account belongs to. Use the fee schedule code, not the display name.

Section 2: Remediation
1. Assign the household fee schedule to each account, or link the account to its household so it inherits the schedule. Use the fee schedule code, not the display name.
2. If the household carries a household rebalancing fee schedule, confirm the rebalance events for the period are present. Keep the household rebalancing fee separate from the asset-based fee.

Section 3: Re-run
1. Re-run the billing period for the affected households only. Use the fee schedule code, not the display name.
2. Confirm the run status moves to completed and attach the run id to the incident ticket. Keep the household rebalancing fee separate from the asset-based fee.
