Migrating a household to a new fee schedule (Private client group, v7)

Owner: Northwind Capital billing operations. Internal reference: NW-CANARY-7731-PR033.

Section 1: Preparation
1. Confirm the client signed the fee schedule amendment and that FIRM_ADMIN approval is recorded. Keep the household rebalancing fee separate from the asset-based fee.
2. Note the old and new fee schedule codes. Keep the household rebalancing fee separate from the asset-based fee.

Section 2: Migration
1. End-date the old fee schedule on the last day of the current billing period. Use the fee schedule code, not the display name.
2. Assign the new fee schedule from the first day of the next billing period. Only OPS or FIRM_ADMIN users can perform this step.
   The household rebalancing fee terms move with the schedule.

Section 3: Verification
1. Run a billing preview and confirm no period is billed under both schedules. Keep the household rebalancing fee separate from the asset-based fee.
2. File the amendment with the client billing profile. Only OPS or FIRM_ADMIN users can perform this step.
