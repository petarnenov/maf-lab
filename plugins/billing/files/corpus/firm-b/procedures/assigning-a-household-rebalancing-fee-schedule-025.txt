Assigning a household rebalancing fee schedule (Central region, v6)

Owner: Northwind Capital billing operations. Internal reference: NW-CANARY-7731-PR025.

Section 1: Preparation
1. Open the household record in the Northwind Capital billing console and confirm the household has at least two linked accounts. Use the fee schedule code, not the display name.
2. Check which fee schedule is currently assigned and note its code in the change ticket. Only OPS or FIRM_ADMIN users can perform this step.

Section 2: Assignment
1. Select the household rebalancing fee schedule agreed with the client in the advisory agreement. Use the fee schedule code, not the display name.
2. Set the effective date to the first day of the next billing period. Use the fee schedule code, not the display name.
   Do not backdate a fee schedule without FIRM_ADMIN approval.
3. Save the assignment and confirm the household rebalancing fee line appears in the fee preview.

Section 3: Validation
1. Run a billing preview for the household and compare the household rebalancing fee to the expected amount. Keep the household rebalancing fee separate from the asset-based fee.
2. Record the internal reference code in the ticket and close it.
