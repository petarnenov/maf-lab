Voiding and reissuing an invoice

Use this procedure when an approved invoice contains an error that cannot be fixed by a credit on the next invoice.

Section 1: Assess
1. Confirm the nature of the error and whether a credit or adjustment on the next invoice would be sufficient.
   Voiding is reserved for errors that make the invoice itself misleading, such as the wrong client address.
2. Check whether the invoice has already been included in a released custodian fee file.
   If so, reconcile the deduction first so the collected amount is known.

Section 2: Void
1. A FIRM_ADMIN opens the invoice and selects "Void".
2. Enter a void reason; the reason is stored permanently with the invoice number.
3. Confirm that the invoice shows as voided and the number is not reused.

Section 3: Reissue
1. Correct the underlying data, such as the address, schedule assignment, or valuation.
2. Generate a corrective invoice for the same period, which receives a new invoice number.
3. Approve and deliver the corrective invoice, and add a note referencing the voided invoice number.
