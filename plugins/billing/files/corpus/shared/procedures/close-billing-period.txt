Closing a billing period

Closing a period locks it against new runs and marks billing for the period as final. Only a FIRM_ADMIN can close a period.

Section 1: Readiness checks
1. Confirm that the period has exactly one completed billing run and no pending, running, or failed runs.
   Failed runs must be resolved and re-run, or explicitly cancelled with a note.
2. Confirm that all invoices for the period are approved or voided.
3. Confirm that custodian fee deductions have been reconciled and every reconciliation exception is resolved.
4. Review the adjustments report for the period and resolve any credits still in draft.

Section 2: Close
1. Open the billing period page and select "Close period".
2. Review the summary of runs, invoices, total fees, and credits shown on the confirmation screen.
3. Enter a closing note and confirm.
   The period state changes from open to closed and the action is written to the audit log.

Section 3: After closing
1. Export the period billing summary for the firm's finance team.
2. Any later correction for the closed period must be posted as an adjustment in the next open period, referencing the closed period.
3. If a period was closed in error, contact platform support; reopening requires a documented reason.
