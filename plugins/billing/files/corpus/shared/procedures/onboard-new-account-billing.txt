Setting up billing for a new account

Use this procedure when a new advisory account is opened so that it is billed correctly from its first period.

Section 1: Collect information
1. Obtain the signed advisory agreement and note the agreed fee schedule and billing start date.
2. Confirm the account number and custodian match the custodian account master.
3. Determine whether the account belongs to an existing household.

Section 2: Configure
1. Add the account to its household with an effective date equal to the funding date, if applicable.
2. Assign the agreed fee schedule to the account unless it should inherit the household schedule.
3. Set any billing exclusions, such as excluded cash, with the same effective date.
4. If the account pays fees from another account, set the paying account on the household.

Section 3: Verify
1. Run the schedule validation check for the next billing period.
   The account must not appear in the list of accounts without a schedule.
2. Review the estimated fee preview for the account.
3. Note in the account record that billing setup is complete, including your name and the date.
