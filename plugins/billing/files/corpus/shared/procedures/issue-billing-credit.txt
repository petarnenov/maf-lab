Issuing a billing credit

Use this procedure to reduce what a client owes, for example after an overcharge, a service issue, or an advance-billing refund for a closed account.

Section 1: Preparation
1. Confirm the reason for the credit and gather supporting details such as the invoice number and period.
2. Check the firm's credit limit for your role.
   Credits above the limit stay in draft until a FIRM_ADMIN approves them.
3. If you are an ADVISOR, submit a credit request instead; an OPS user will create the credit.

Section 2: Create the credit
1. Open the account or household and select "New credit".
2. Enter the amount, the period it relates to, and a reason code.
   Use the reason code that best matches the cause, for example OVERCHARGE, SERVICE, or ADVANCE-REFUND.
3. Choose whether the credit is applied to the next invoice or refunded through the custodian.
4. Enter an internal note explaining the calculation of the amount.
   The note appears in the audit log but not on the client invoice.

Section 3: Approval
1. Submit the credit; if it is within your limit it is approved automatically.
2. If approval is required, notify a FIRM_ADMIN other than yourself.
   The platform prevents a user from approving a credit they created.

Section 4: Confirm
1. After approval, verify the credit appears as pending on the account's next invoice or in the custodian refund file.
2. Inform the advisor and, where appropriate, the client that the credit has been issued.
