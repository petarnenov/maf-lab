Reviewing a completed billing run before invoice approval

Use this procedure after a billing run completes and before invoices are approved or delivered.

Section 1: Summary checks
1. Compare the total fee for the run with the previous period.
   A change of more than 10% should be explained by market movement or account changes.
2. Compare the account count with the number of billable accounts at the valuation date.
3. Review all warnings, especially fees below minimum and accounts with zero AUM.

Section 2: Sample checks
1. Select at least five households across different fee schedules.
2. Recalculate each selected fee manually from the AUM, schedule, and billable days.
3. Confirm that proration was applied to accounts opened or closed during the period.

Section 3: Sign-off
1. Record the review result on the run with any exceptions found.
2. If issues are found, correct them through adjustments or by voiding and re-running before delivery.
3. Notify the FIRM_ADMIN that the run is ready for invoice approval.
