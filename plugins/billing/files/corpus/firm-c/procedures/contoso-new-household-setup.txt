Setting Up a New Household for Billing

Section 1: Collect Documents
1. Obtain the signed client agreement and confirm the fee schedule and tier named in it.
2. Obtain the custodian account numbers for every account the household is opening.
3. Record the agreement signature date, which is the proration start date for the first invoice.

Section 2: Platform Setup
1. Create the household on the TAMP platform using the primary client's legal name.
2. Link each custodian account to the household as soon as the account number is issued.
3. Assign CONTOSO-FLAT-100 or the schedule named in the agreement to every linked account.
4. Record the flat fee tier on the household record.

Section 3: Verify
1. Compare the number of linked accounts on the platform with the new account paperwork.
2. Run the unassigned-accounts report and confirm none of the new accounts appear.
3. Flag the household for first-invoice review by the operations lead in the next quarterly run.
