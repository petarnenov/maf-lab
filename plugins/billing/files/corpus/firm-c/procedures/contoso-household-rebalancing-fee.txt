Recording a Household Rebalancing Fee

Section 1: Confirm Eligibility
1. Open the household notes and find the advisor's record of the client's rebalancing request.
2. Confirm that the request was client-initiated and outside the semi-annual review.
3. Confirm that the rebalance affected two or more accounts in the household.
   Single-account trades do not qualify for the household rebalancing fee.

Section 2: Check for Waivers
1. Check the billing queue for a waiver request from the advisor.
2. If a waiver was approved by the operations lead, record the fee as a zero adjustment with the reason "waived" and the waiver reason.
3. If a waiver is pending, hold the adjustment until the operations lead decides.

Section 3: Enter the Adjustment
1. Create a debit adjustment on the household for the current open billing period.
2. Select the reason code for household rebalancing fee and reference the date of the client request in the note.
3. Set the debit account to the largest account in the household unless the client instructed otherwise.
4. Confirm that the adjustment appears in the pending adjustments list for the next quarterly run.
