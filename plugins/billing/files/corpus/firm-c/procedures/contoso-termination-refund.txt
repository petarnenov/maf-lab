Processing a Termination Refund

Section 1: Record the Termination
1. File the client's written termination notice in the household documents.
2. Determine the termination effective date, which is the date the notice was received unless a later date is stated.
3. Mark every household account as closed on the platform with the effective date.

Section 2: Calculate the Refund
1. Confirm the quarterly installment paid for the current quarter under CONTOSO-FLAT-100.
2. Let the platform calculate the daily prorated refund for the remaining days in the quarter.
   Check the platform result by hand for any refund above the review threshold.
3. Exclude any household rebalancing fee already charged for a completed rebalance.

Section 3: Pay the Refund
1. If any account remains open at the custodian, enter the refund as a credit adjustment for the next run.
2. If all accounts have transferred away, ask the operations lead to request a check refund.
3. Record the refund amount and calculation in the termination log.
