Changing a Household Fee Tier

Section 1: Request
1. Receive the advisor's written tier change proposal with the reason.
2. Confirm the new tier exists in CONTOSO-FLAT-100 and note the new annual fee.

Section 2: Notify the Client
1. For an increase, send the client a letter at least 30 days before the start of the quarter in which the change takes effect.
2. For a decrease, note that the change applies from the next run.

Section 3: Update the Platform
1. Update the tier on the household record with the effective quarter.
2. Confirm that the next run's preview shows the new installment amount.
3. File the client letter and the advisor proposal in the household documents.
