Correcting a Stale Valuation at Contoso Advisors

Section 1: Identify
1. Open the run or pre-run report that shows AUM-STALE and list the affected accounts.
2. Check the custodian file date for each account to confirm the price is out of date.
3. Note whether the affected households are flat-fee or legacy asset-based.
   For flat-fee households the stale value only affects allocation; for legacy households it changes the fee.

Section 2: Correct
1. Wait up to one business day for the custodian to deliver a corrected file.
2. If no corrected file arrives, request a manual valuation from the custodian service desk.
3. Enter the manual valuation on the platform with the source and date in the note field.

Section 3: Confirm
1. Refresh the valuation report and confirm the AUM-STALE warning has cleared.
2. Start or re-run the billing run.
3. Record the stale valuation incident in the exception log.
