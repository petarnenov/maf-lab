Handling a Client Billing Dispute

Section 1: Intake
1. Log the dispute in the exception log on the day it is received.
2. Record the household name, invoice number, and the client's description.

Section 2: Investigate
1. Compare the invoice to the client agreement, schedule assignment, and adjustments.
2. For household rebalancing fee disputes, confirm the client request is documented and check for waivers.
3. For proration disputes, recalculate days from the funding or termination date.

Section 3: Respond
1. If the invoice is wrong, enter a credit and route it for approval.
2. Send the client a written response within ten business days.
3. Close the dispute in the exception log once resolved.
