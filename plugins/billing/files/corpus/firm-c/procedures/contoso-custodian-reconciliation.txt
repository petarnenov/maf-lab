Reconciling Custodian Fee Deductions

Section 1: Gather Reports
1. Download the custodian fee processing report two business days after submitting the fee file.
2. Export the invoice allocation report for the same billing run from the platform.

Section 2: Match
1. Match each custodian debit to the platform allocation by account number and amount.
2. List any debit on the custodian report with no matching allocation, and any allocation with no debit.
3. For each CUSTODIAN-MISMATCH warning on the run, check whether the account was closed, transferred, or re-registered.

Section 3: Resolve
1. For rejected debits, ask the operations lead whether to re-bill another household account or invoice the client.
2. For differences above one dollar, document the cause and the correcting adjustment.
3. Save the completed reconciliation to the quarterly billing binder.
