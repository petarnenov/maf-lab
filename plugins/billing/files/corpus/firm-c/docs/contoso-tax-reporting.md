# Advisory Fees and Tax Reporting

## Year-End Summary

Contoso Advisors provides each household with a year-end summary of advisory fees paid during the calendar year. The summary lists every installment under CONTOSO-FLAT-100, every household rebalancing fee, and every credit, grouped by account. Because fees are billed quarterly in advance, the fee debited on January 2 pays for the first quarter of the new year, and the fee debited on October 1 of the prior year pays for the fourth quarter. The summary uses debit dates, which match the custodian's tax documents.

## Account Types

### Taxable Accounts

For taxable accounts, the summary shows fees paid so the client and their accountant can decide how to treat them under current tax rules. Contoso Advisors does not provide tax advice about deductibility.

### Retirement Accounts

Fees debited from IRAs are paid with pre-tax money inside the account. The firm allocates the household flat fee across accounts by value, which means retirement accounts often pay part of the fee. Clients who prefer to pay the full fee from taxable accounts can request a custom allocation; the operations lead records the instruction on the household.

## Corrections

If a fee is corrected after year-end, the correction appears on the following year's summary with a note referring to the original period. Contoso Advisors does not reissue prior-year summaries unless the error is material.
