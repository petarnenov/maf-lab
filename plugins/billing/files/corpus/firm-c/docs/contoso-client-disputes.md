# Handling Client Billing Disputes

## Intake

A billing dispute is any client complaint that an invoice amount is wrong or unexpected. At Contoso Advisors, disputes usually concern the household rebalancing fee, a proration amount for a newly funded account, or confusion about quarterly-in-advance billing. Disputes can arrive through the advisor, the client portal, or directly by email to the operations desk. Whoever receives the dispute records it in the exception log on the same day, with the household name, invoice number, and the client's description of the problem.

## Investigation

### Review the Invoice

The operations associate compares the invoice with the client agreement, the schedule assignment, and any adjustments on the household. For rebalancing fee disputes, the associate checks that the client request is documented and that no waiver applied. For proration disputes, the associate recalculates the days and confirms the account funding date.

### Decide

If the invoice is wrong, the associate prepares a credit adjustment and the operations lead approves it. If the invoice is correct, the advisor explains the charge to the client, using the fee disclosure language from the agreement.

## Closure

Every dispute receives a written response within ten business days. The response states whether a credit will be issued and in which billing period. The dispute is closed in the exception log once the client acknowledges the response or thirty days pass without further contact.
