# Contoso Advisors Billing Glossary

## Fee Terms

### Flat Fee

A fixed annual advisory fee charged per household regardless of asset value. At Contoso Advisors the flat fee is defined by the CONTOSO-FLAT-100 schedule and is collected in four equal quarterly installments. The flat fee covers ongoing investment management, scheduled rebalancing, and annual planning meetings. It does not cover third-party fund expenses, custodian charges, or the household rebalancing fee for client-requested out-of-cycle rebalances, which are disclosed separately.

### Household Rebalancing Fee

A one-time charge applied when a client requests an out-of-cycle rebalance affecting two or more accounts in a household. It is billed as an adjustment on the next quarterly run and can be waived by the operations lead for qualifying reasons.

### Installment

One quarter of the annual flat fee. Installments are billed in advance at the start of each calendar quarter and allocated across the household's accounts by value.

## Operational Terms

### Billing Run

A platform process that calculates fees for all Contoso Advisors accounts for one billing period. A run moves through pending, running, and then completed or failed. A failed run shows a failure code such as FS-REQUIRED or AUM-STALE.

### Allocation

The process of spreading a household-level fee across the household's accounts. Contoso Advisors allocates in proportion to market value at the start of the quarter, with zero-balance accounts excluded.

### Credit

A negative adjustment that returns money to a client, for example the unused portion of a prepaid quarter after termination.
