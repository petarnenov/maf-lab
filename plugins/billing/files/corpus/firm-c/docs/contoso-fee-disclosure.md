# Fee Disclosure Practices

## Client Agreement

Every Contoso Advisors client agreement states the fee schedule code, the annual fee amount, and the billing frequency. For flat-fee clients the agreement names CONTOSO-FLAT-100 and states that fees are billed quarterly in advance. The agreement also describes the household rebalancing fee, the conditions under which it applies, and the waiver process. Clients initial the fee section to confirm they have read it. Agreements are stored in the document vault and linked to the household on the TAMP platform.

## Ongoing Disclosure

### Quarterly Invoices

Each quarterly invoice shows the installment amount, the period covered, the accounts debited, and any adjustments such as proration or a household rebalancing fee. Invoices are delivered through the client portal on the day the run is approved. Paper copies are mailed only on request.

### Annual Fee Summary

Every January, Contoso Advisors sends each household an annual fee summary showing total advisory fees paid in the prior year, broken down by account. The summary helps clients compare what they paid with what the agreement says. Differences reported by clients are investigated by the operations lead within ten business days.

## Changes to Fees

Any increase in the flat fee requires at least 30 days' written notice before the start of the quarter in which it takes effect. Clients may terminate without penalty during the notice period. Decreases can take effect immediately and are communicated in the next invoice.
