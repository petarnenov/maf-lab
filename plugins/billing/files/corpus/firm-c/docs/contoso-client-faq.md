# Contoso Advisors Client Billing FAQ

## General Questions

### How am I billed?

Most Contoso Advisors clients pay a flat annual advisory fee under the CONTOSO-FLAT-100 schedule. The annual fee is split into four quarterly installments, and each installment is billed at the start of the quarter it covers. Your quarterly statement from the custodian shows the fee as a single debit per account. If your household has several accounts, the installment is spread across them in proportion to their value at the start of the quarter, so the total you pay stays the same.

### Why is my fee charged in advance?

Contoso Advisors bills quarterly in advance, which is common for flat-fee advisory relationships. Paying in advance means the fee covers the upcoming quarter of planning and management. If you end the relationship during a quarter, the unused portion of the fee is returned to you as a credit or refund.

## Special Charges

### What is the household rebalancing fee?

If you ask us to rebalance your household portfolio outside the regular semi-annual review, a small household rebalancing fee may apply. Rebalances we initiate ourselves are always included in your flat fee. Your advisor will tell you before any fee is charged, and the fee appears as a separate line on your next invoice.

### Can a fee be waived?

Yes. Advisors can request a waiver for the first rebalance after you join, for hardship, or for major life events such as retirement. Waivers are approved by our operations lead and recorded in your household file so the invoice shows the charge at zero.

## Contact

For billing questions, contact your advisor or the Contoso Advisors operations desk. We respond to billing inquiries within two business days and will send a corrected invoice if an error is confirmed.
