# Minimum Fee and Small Households

## Minimum Annual Fee

Contoso Advisors sets a minimum annual fee equal to the Essential tier of CONTOSO-FLAT-100. Because the schedule is flat, most households already pay at least the minimum. The minimum matters mainly for legacy asset-based households under CONTOSO-AUM-LEGACY, whose percentage fee on a small balance can fall below the Essential tier amount. In that case the platform bills the minimum instead of the calculated percentage, and the invoice shows a line explaining the minimum fee adjustment.

## Small Household Considerations

### Young Professionals

The firm occasionally accepts young professional households with small balances at a reduced fee as part of a mentoring program. These households are placed on CONTOSO-FLAT-100 with a documented fee discount rather than a separate schedule. The discount is recorded on the household and reviewed annually.

### Family Members of Existing Clients

Adult children of existing clients may be billed under the parents' household by agreement, in which case no additional flat fee is charged. The operations lead must approve this arrangement in writing.

## Operational Notes

The minimum fee is applied per household per quarter, as one quarter of the annual minimum. Prorated first-quarter invoices apply the minimum on a prorated basis, so a household that joins halfway through a quarter pays half of the quarterly minimum. Minimum fee adjustments are never subject to the household rebalancing fee waiver process.
