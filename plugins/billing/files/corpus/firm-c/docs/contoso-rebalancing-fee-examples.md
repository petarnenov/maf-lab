# Household Rebalancing Fee Examples

## Charged Examples

### Client-Requested Risk Change

A Contoso Advisors client calls in the middle of a quarter and asks to move the household from a growth allocation to a conservative allocation across a joint account and two IRAs. The request is outside the semi-annual review and touches three accounts. The household rebalancing fee applies. The advisor documents the request, informs the client of the fee, and the operations associate enters a debit adjustment for the next quarterly run under the CONTOSO-FLAT-100 household.

### Large Deposit

A client deposits an inheritance into a taxable account and asks the firm to rebalance the whole household to account for the new cash. Because the client asked for a household-level rebalance, the fee applies.

## Exempt Examples

### Firm Model Change

Contoso Advisors updates its model portfolio and rebalances all households on the model. The rebalance is initiated by the firm, so no household rebalancing fee is charged to anyone.

### Single-Account Trade

A client asks to raise cash in one IRA for a required minimum distribution. Only one account is affected, so the trade is not a household rebalance and no fee applies.

## Waived Example

A client retires mid-quarter and asks to restructure the household for income. The advisor requests a life-event waiver, the operations lead approves it, and the fee is recorded at zero on the next invoice with the note "waived - retirement".
