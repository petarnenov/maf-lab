# Terminations and Refunds

## Policy

Because Contoso Advisors bills quarterly in advance, a client who ends the advisory relationship mid-quarter has prepaid for days the firm will not serve. The firm's policy is to refund the unused portion on a daily prorated basis. The termination date is the date Contoso Advisors receives written notice, or a later date stated in the notice. No termination fee is charged. Any household rebalancing fee already billed for a completed rebalance is not refunded, because the work has been performed.

## Calculation

### Daily Proration

The refund equals the quarterly installment multiplied by the number of remaining days in the quarter after the termination date, divided by the total days in the quarter. The calculation uses actual calendar days. For example, a termination on the 60th day of a 91-day quarter returns 31/91 of the installment. The platform performs the calculation automatically when the account is marked as closed with an effective date.

### Partial Household Terminations

If a household closes only some of its accounts, the flat fee under CONTOSO-FLAT-100 does not change, because it is charged per household. The remaining accounts absorb the allocation from the next quarter onward. No refund is due unless the entire household leaves.

## Payment of Refunds

Refunds are issued as billing credits on the next run if any account remains open at the custodian. If all accounts have been transferred away, the operations lead requests a check refund through the firm's accounting system. Every refund is recorded in the termination log with the calculation attached.
