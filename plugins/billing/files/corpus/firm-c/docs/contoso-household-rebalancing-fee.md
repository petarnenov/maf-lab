# Household Rebalancing Fee Policy

## Overview

Contoso Advisors charges a household rebalancing fee when a client requests an out-of-cycle rebalance of the household portfolio. Regular rebalancing performed by the firm as part of ongoing management is included in the flat fee and never triggers this charge. The policy exists because ad-hoc rebalances requested by clients, for example after a large deposit or a change in risk tolerance, require additional analysis, trading and paperwork. The fee is modest and is disclosed in the client agreement and in the firm's fee brochure.

## When the Fee Applies

### Qualifying Requests

The household rebalancing fee applies when the client initiates a rebalance outside the scheduled semi-annual review, and the rebalance touches two or more accounts in the household. A single-account trade request is not treated as a household rebalance. Rebalances caused by the firm's own model changes, tax-loss harvesting or corrective trades are always exempt, and the advisor must note the reason in the household record so the operations team can confirm the exemption.

### Waivers

An advisor may request a waiver for hardship, for the first rebalance after onboarding, or when the rebalance is related to a life event such as retirement or the death of a spouse. Waivers are approved by the operations lead and logged in the household notes. A waived fee is still recorded on the billing run at zero so the audit trail is complete.

## Billing Treatment

The household rebalancing fee is billed as a one-time adjustment on the next quarterly run, not as part of the CONTOSO-FLAT-100 installment. It is shown as a separate invoice line. The fee is debited from the largest account in the household unless the client has instructed otherwise. Compliance tracks these adjustments under internal reference CONTOSO-CANARY-2290.
