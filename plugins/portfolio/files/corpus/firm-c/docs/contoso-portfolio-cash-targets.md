# Contoso Advisors Cash Targets

## Cash Targets by Model

Contoso Advisors sets a strategic cash target in both of its models: 5% in CONTOSO-GROWTH and 15% in CONTOSO-INCOME. The difference reflects purpose. In CONTOSO-GROWTH the 5% is a working balance for rebalancing and incidental withdrawals. In CONTOSO-INCOME the 15% is a distribution reserve: it is sized to cover roughly twelve months of a typical income client's planned withdrawals, so that a market decline does not force the sale of bonds or equities to pay the client.

## Sweep Vehicle Election

Contoso elects a government money market fund as the sweep vehicle at its custodian for all managed accounts, rather than the custodian's bank deposit program. The principals chose the money market fund because its yield has historically tracked short-term Treasury rates more closely. The sweep balance is reported as Cash on the platform and counts toward each model's cash target and cash drift.

## Replenishing the Income Reserve

Monthly distributions from CONTOSO-INCOME accounts draw the cash weight down steadily. Contoso does not wait for cash to fall below its tolerance band (10% under the 5-point tolerance) before acting. Instead, at each quarterly review the operations lead tops cash back up to 15% by trimming whichever of Core bonds or US equity is most overweight, and bond coupons and dividends are left in cash rather than reinvested. This keeps a full year of distributions on hand while holding trading to four times a year for most income accounts.

## Handling Excess Cash

When a contribution lands in a CONTOSO-GROWTH account, cash often jumps well above its 10% upper band. The operations lead invests the excess within five business days by buying the underweight asset classes. For CONTOSO-INCOME accounts, cash above 20% is invested unless the client has told the advisor about a planned large withdrawal, in which case an account-level reserve is recorded so the excess is not flagged as drift until the withdrawal date.

## Cash in Valuation and Performance

Cash is included in the quarter-end market value of every Contoso account. For the majority of Contoso households, which pay a flat annual fee, the size of the cash balance has no effect on the fee. Cash does affect performance: the high cash target in CONTOSO-INCOME lowers expected return in rising markets, and Contoso's quarterly reports show the sweep yield and the cash drag relative to the model benchmark so clients understand the trade-off between return and a reliable income reserve.
