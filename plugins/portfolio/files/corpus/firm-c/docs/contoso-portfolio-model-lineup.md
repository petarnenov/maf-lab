# Contoso Advisors Model Portfolio Lineup

## Two Models for a Concentrated Book

Contoso Advisors keeps its model lineup deliberately small to match its small, concentrated client base. Two models are available: CONTOSO-GROWTH for clients who are accumulating wealth and CONTOSO-INCOME for clients who draw regular income from their portfolios. Both use a 5-point drift tolerance. The operations lead maintains the model definitions on the platform, and changes to target weights are agreed by the firm's two principals before they are entered.

## CONTOSO-GROWTH

CONTOSO-GROWTH targets US equity 55%, International equity 20%, Core bonds 20% and Cash 5%. It has the highest equity allocation Contoso offers and a heavier US tilt than most balanced models, reflecting the principals' preference for domestic large-cap exposure. With a 5-point tolerance, the US equity band runs from 50% to 60%, and that band is the one most often breached in strong equity markets. The benchmark is a 55/20/20/5 blend of US equity, developed ex-US equity, aggregate bond and Treasury bill indexes.

## CONTOSO-INCOME

CONTOSO-INCOME targets Core bonds 55%, US equity 30% and Cash 15%. It holds no international equity, a deliberate simplification for income clients who prefer a portfolio they can follow easily and whose dividend income comes mainly from US companies. The 15% cash target is the highest of any Contoso model and funds monthly distributions; how that cash is sized and replenished is covered in Contoso's cash targets guidance. The 5-point tolerance gives a Core bonds band of 50% to 60%.

## Assigning and Reviewing Models

Contoso assigns a model at onboarding based on whether the client needs regular withdrawals: clients with a monthly or quarterly income need go to CONTOSO-INCOME, everyone else to CONTOSO-GROWTH. Each client's model is reconfirmed at the annual review. Because Contoso's book is small, the operations lead reviews every account's drift report individually each month rather than relying only on the automated threshold queue, and rebalances any account outside tolerance within five business days.

## Model Choice and Fees

Most Contoso households pay a flat annual advisory fee, so their fee does not depend on which model they hold or on changes in their account value. For the few legacy households on an asset-based schedule, the quarter-end valuation handed to billing includes every asset class in the model, including cash. Moving a client between CONTOSO-GROWTH and CONTOSO-INCOME never changes the fee schedule itself.
