Correcting a wrong quarter-end price before billing runs

Section 1: Confirm the Error
1. Identify the security, the account or accounts that hold it, and the quarter-end date.
2. Compare the stored quarter-end price with at least one independent source (exchange close, fund NAV, vendor evaluated price or custodian statement).
3. Calculate the effect on each affected account's quarter-end market value in US dollars.

Section 2: Coordinate with the Billing Desk
1. Notify the billing desk before changing anything, listing the affected accounts, the old and new quarter-end values and the reason.
   Billing reads the quarter-end valuation as billable AUM, so the desk must know before the run starts or must hold it.
2. Ask the billing desk to confirm whether the quarterly billing run has already read the value.
3. If billing has not run, agree that the run will wait until the correction is locked.
   If billing has already run, the correction is made as a new version and the billing desk decides on a rerun or adjustment.

Section 3: Correct the Price
1. Enter the corrected price for the quarter-end date with the source and reason.
2. Confirm the valuation history retains the original price and the correction.
3. Relock the quarter-end valuation and confirm the affected accounts show the corrected market value.

Section 4: Downstream Checks
1. Rerun the quarter-end drift report for the affected accounts; the corrected weights may move an account inside or outside tolerance.
2. Regenerate quarterly performance for the affected accounts so the ending value matches the corrected valuation.
3. Tell the billing desk the correction is locked so the run can proceed.
4. Record the correction, the approver and the billing desk confirmation in the valuation notes.
