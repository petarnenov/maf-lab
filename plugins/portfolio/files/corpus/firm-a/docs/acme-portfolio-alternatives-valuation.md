# Alternatives Valuation at Acme Wealth Partners

## Where Alternatives Appear

Alternatives appear only in ACME-GROWTH-80-20, where they carry a 10% target weight. The Alternatives sleeve holds interests in private credit and private real estate funds, plus a small position in a daily-liquidity multi-strategy fund. The private fund interests cannot be traded on demand, and their values are reported by the fund managers rather than by an exchange, which makes valuation and drift for this asset class different from the rest of the model.

## Valuation from Capital Account Statements

Private fund interests are valued from the most recent capital account statement issued by the fund manager, usually quarterly and usually four to eight weeks after quarter end. When the quarter-end valuation is struck, the latest available statement is typically for the prior quarter. That value is carried forward, adjusted for any capital calls and distributions made since the statement date, and flagged as carried forward in the valuation record with the statement date shown. The daily-liquidity fund is priced at its published net asset value like any mutual fund.

## Carried-Forward Review Before Billing

Because quarter-end AUM for ACME-GROWTH-80-20 accounts includes the Alternatives sleeve, carried-forward values flow into billable AUM. Any single carried-forward position over $1,000,000 must be reviewed by a FIRM_ADMIN before the quarter-end valuation is handed to billing. The reviewer confirms that capital calls and distributions have been applied and that no newer statement has arrived. When the new statement arrives later, the difference is recorded as a valuation update for the following quarter rather than a correction of the locked quarter-end value, unless the difference exceeds 5% of the position.

## Drift Treatment for an Illiquid Sleeve

The 7.5-point drift tolerance for ACME-GROWTH-80-20 applies to Alternatives like any other asset class, but rebalancing an overweight Alternatives position cannot be done by selling the private funds. When Alternatives are overweight, Acme rebalances by trimming the multi-strategy fund and directing new contributions to other asset classes. When Alternatives are underweight, the gap is filled through the multi-strategy fund until the next private fund subscription window. Drift reports mark the Alternatives weight as based on carried-forward values so that portfolio managers can see how stale the figure is.

## Performance Effects of Lagged Values

Because private fund values lag, the Alternatives sleeve's time-weighted return for a quarter often reflects the prior quarter's market. Acme's quarterly performance reports footnote this lag for every ACME-GROWTH-80-20 account and compare the sleeve with a lagged benchmark so that the comparison is on the same timing.
