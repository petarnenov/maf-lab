# Acme Wealth Partners Model Portfolio Lineup

## The Three Core Models

Acme Wealth Partners manages its discretionary accounts to three core model portfolios: ACME-CONSERVATIVE-30-70, ACME-BALANCED-60-40 and ACME-GROWTH-80-20. The number pairs in each code describe the approximate split between growth assets and defensive assets. Every new discretionary account is assigned one of the three based on the client's risk questionnaire score and investment policy statement, and the Acme investment committee reviews the lineup twice a year. Custom models are not offered; clients who need a different mix are served through a sleeve within one of the core models.

## ACME-BALANCED-60-40

ACME-BALANCED-60-40 is Acme's default model and holds the largest share of client assets. Target weights are US equity 40%, International equity 20%, Core bonds 35% and Cash 5%, for 60% growth assets and 40% defensive assets. The drift tolerance is 5 percentage points, so the US equity band runs from 35% to 45% and the cash band from 0% to 10%. The blended benchmark uses the same weights applied to a broad US equity index, a developed ex-US equity index, an aggregate bond index and three-month Treasury bills.

## ACME-CONSERVATIVE-30-70

ACME-CONSERVATIVE-30-70 serves retirees, retirement plans that are paying benefits, and clients with short time horizons. Target weights are US equity 20%, International equity 10%, Core bonds 60% and Cash 10%. The higher cash target funds scheduled distributions, which Acme typically pays monthly or quarterly from this model, without forcing bond sales. The drift tolerance is 5 percentage points. Because bonds dominate the model, the Core bonds band (55% to 65%) is the one most often breached after a sharp move in rates.

## ACME-GROWTH-80-20

ACME-GROWTH-80-20 is for long-horizon clients and business entities with no near-term liquidity need. Target weights are US equity 50%, International equity 25%, Alternatives 10% and Core bonds 15%, with no strategic cash target; operating cash is held only as the rebalancing buffer. It is the only Acme model with an Alternatives allocation, which is valued as described in Acme's alternatives valuation guidance. The drift tolerance is wider, at 7.5 percentage points, because equity volatility would otherwise trigger frequent trading and because the Alternatives sleeve cannot be traded quickly.

## Choosing and Changing Models

Advisors choose the model from the risk score bands set by the investment committee: low scores map to Conservative, mid-range scores to Balanced and high scores to Growth. An advisor may place a client one model more conservative than the score suggests without committee approval, but never one model more aggressive. Model changes for an account follow the platform procedure for moving an account to a different model portfolio and take effect on the first business day of the following month, so that the month-end valuation and drift report line up with the switch.
