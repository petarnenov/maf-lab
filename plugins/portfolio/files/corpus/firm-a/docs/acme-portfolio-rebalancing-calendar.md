# Acme Wealth Partners Rebalancing Calendar

## Quarterly Calendar Review

Every discretionary Acme account is reviewed once per calendar quarter, whether or not it is outside tolerance. The review happens in the second and third weeks of the first month of each quarter, after the quarter-end valuation has been locked and handed to billing. Reviewing after the handoff means that no calendar rebalance trades are pending on the quarter-end date, so the quarter-end drift report and quarter-end AUM describe a settled portfolio. During the review the portfolio manager for each model generates proposals for every account more than 2 percentage points away from any target, even if still inside its tolerance band.

## Weekly Threshold Checks

In addition to the quarterly review, Acme runs a threshold check every Monday using the prior Friday's valuation. Any account with an asset class outside its model's tolerance band, 5 points for ACME-BALANCED-60-40 and ACME-CONSERVATIVE-30-70 and 7.5 points for ACME-GROWTH-80-20, is placed on the rebalance queue. Portfolio managers work the queue by Wednesday. Accounts that were rebalanced within the previous ten business days are held on the queue for one more week unless the drift exceeds twice the tolerance, which avoids trading back and forth in volatile markets.

## Approval Rules at Acme

Rebalance proposals are approved by the portfolio manager responsible for the model. Proposals with a total trade value over $2,000,000, or any proposal that sells from the Alternatives sleeve, need a second approval from the chief investment officer or a delegate. Advisors can request a rebalance for their client but cannot approve one.

## Quarter-End Blackout

Acme does not submit discretionary rebalance trades during the last two business days of a quarter. Trades placed then could settle across the quarter-end date and leave pending cash or unsettled positions in the quarter-end valuation. Client-directed contributions and withdrawals are still processed during the blackout, and a threshold breach found during it is queued for the first business day of the new quarter.

## Tax-Aware Settings for Taxable Accounts

Taxable Acme accounts use loss harvesting and avoid realizing short-term gains during both the quarterly review and weekly checks. When full correction would realize more than $50,000 of net gains, the proposal is limited to bringing each asset class back inside its band rather than all the way to target, and the advisor is notified so the client can decide whether to accept the remaining drift.
