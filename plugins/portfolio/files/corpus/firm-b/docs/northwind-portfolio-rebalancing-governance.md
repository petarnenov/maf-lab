# Northwind Capital Rebalancing Governance for Pension and Endowment Clients

## Role of the Investment Committee

Northwind Capital's investment committee governs rebalancing for every pension and endowment account. The committee meets monthly and on call. It approves the model portfolios and their tolerance bands, reviews the monthly drift report, and approves any rebalance outside the pre-authorized limits described below. The committee also reports to each client's own board or trustees once a quarter, so rebalancing decisions must be traceable to a committee minute or a pre-authorization.

## Pre-Authorized Threshold Rebalancing

To avoid delaying routine corrections, the committee pre-authorizes portfolio managers to rebalance an account back to target when any asset class is outside its tolerance band, 3 points for NW-LIABILITY-DRIVEN and 5 points for NW-ENDOWMENT-70-30, provided the total trade value is under 5% of the account's market value. Drift is checked daily on the prior business day's valuation. Pre-authorized rebalances are executed within two business days of the breach and reported at the next committee meeting.

## Rebalances Requiring Committee Approval

A rebalance needs explicit committee approval when its trade value is 5% or more of the account's market value, when it is triggered by a change to the model's target weights, or when it would reduce the Long-duration bonds allocation of a NW-LIABILITY-DRIVEN account below its lower band for any period during execution. For pension plans, the committee also approves any rebalance that changes the hedge ratio by more than five percentage points, even if the account remains inside tolerance. Approval is recorded in the minutes with the proposal, the expected drift after trading and the vote.

## Liability-Driven Accounts: Hedge Ratio Monitoring

For NW-LIABILITY-DRIVEN accounts, drift is monitored alongside the hedge ratio, which compares the interest-rate sensitivity of the bond allocation with that of the plan's liabilities. A fall in equity markets pushes the bond weight up and the hedge ratio with it; a rise in rates reduces bond value and lowers both. The narrow 3-point band keeps the hedge ratio within the range the plan's trustees have accepted. Cash inflows from employer contributions are invested first in whichever asset class is most underweight, and benefit payments are drawn from the 10% cash allocation.

## Endowment Accounts: Spending and Grants

For NW-ENDOWMENT-70-30 accounts, the quarterly spending distribution is paid from the 5% cash allocation, which is then refilled at the next threshold or calendar rebalance by selling the most overweight asset class. Large one-time grants approved by the foundation's board are pre-funded by raising cash over the preceding weeks, so the grant does not force a single large sale. Grants and spending are recorded as withdrawals, which lower market value and quarter-end AUM but are excluded from time-weighted return.

## Documentation and Audit Trail

Every rebalance record at Northwind holds the triggering drift report, the proposal, the approver or pre-authorization reference and the post-trade drift. Records are retained for seven years and are made available to clients' auditors on request.
