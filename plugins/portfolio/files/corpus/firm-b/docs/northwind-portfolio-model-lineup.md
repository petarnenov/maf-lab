# Northwind Capital Model Portfolio Lineup

## Institutional Focus

Northwind Capital manages assets primarily for institutions and charitable entities: defined benefit pension plans, foundations, endowments and charitable trusts. Its model lineup is correspondingly short and built around the obligations of those clients rather than around individual risk scores. Two models are in use on the platform: NW-LIABILITY-DRIVEN for pension plans with defined liabilities, and NW-ENDOWMENT-70-30 for foundations and endowments that spend a percentage of assets each year. Each model is owned by the Northwind investment committee, which approves every change to target weights or tolerance.

## NW-LIABILITY-DRIVEN

NW-LIABILITY-DRIVEN is designed for pension plans whose primary goal is to hedge the interest-rate sensitivity of their benefit obligations. Target weights are Long-duration bonds 60%, US equity 20%, International equity 10% and Cash 10%. The long-duration bond allocation is chosen so that its duration approximates the plan's liability duration; the equity allocation provides growth to close any funding gap; and the cash allocation covers monthly benefit payments and collateral needs. The drift tolerance is 3 percentage points, the narrowest on the platform, because a drift of even a few points in the bond allocation changes the plan's hedge ratio materially.

## NW-ENDOWMENT-70-30

NW-ENDOWMENT-70-30 serves foundations, endowments and charitable trusts with long horizons and an annual spending policy. Target weights are US equity 45%, International equity 25%, Core bonds 25% and Cash 5%, giving 70% growth assets and 30% defensive assets. The drift tolerance is 5 percentage points. The cash target is sized to hold roughly one quarter's spending distribution, and the rebalancer draws on it before selling any other asset class when a grant payment is made.

## Blended Benchmarks

Each model has a policy benchmark approved by the investment committee. The NW-LIABILITY-DRIVEN benchmark pairs a long government and credit index with equity indexes in the model weights, and the committee also reviews a liability-relative comparison each quarter showing the change in funded status. The NW-ENDOWMENT-70-30 benchmark uses equity, aggregate bond and Treasury bill indexes in the model weights, and it is compared with the foundation's spending rate plus inflation over rolling five-year periods.

## Assigning Clients to a Model

A new client is assigned a model only after its investment policy statement has been adopted by the client's own board or trustees and accepted by Northwind. Pension plans are assigned NW-LIABILITY-DRIVEN unless the plan is frozen and fully funded, in which case the committee may approve a custom long-duration sleeve. Foundations and trusts are assigned NW-ENDOWMENT-70-30. Northwind does not move an account between models on advisor judgment alone; every reassignment requires investment committee approval recorded in the committee minutes.
